IS2222 – POS Product Search Fix – 9 Sep 2026

Issue
- POS / Sales: after entering a product and clicking Search, the product was not displayed.

Root causes corrected
1. POS Sales product filter relied on session('business.id') only. The host ERP commonly stores the active business in user.business_id, so valid business-linked POS products could be hidden.
2. Historical POS SQL contains two compatible product schemas: sell_price/stock_qty and selling_price/current_stock. Sales search assumed only the first schema.
3. Browser could keep the previous POS JS for 24 hours because the asset URL version was unchanged.

Changes
- POSCartService now uses POSBaseService businessId() resolution and only applies business filtering when a valid business id is known.
- Search, price lookup and barcode lookup support both historical pos_products column layouts.
- Deleted/inactive products are excluded when those columns exist.
- Sales Workspace search uses the same tenant/schema-safe rules.
- Search/Scan buttons explicitly use type=button.
- Product-search JS now handles HTTP/JSON failures visibly instead of silently leaving an empty grid.
- POS JS asset version changed to is2222-20260909 to force browsers to load the corrected script.

Database
- No SQL/database change required.
