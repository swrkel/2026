POS Standalone S385 - Enterprise Reliability & Disaster Recovery

Purpose
- Final hardening layer for the POS online/offline synchronization platform.
- Adds reliability audit dashboard, disaster recovery checklist, integrity checks, and stress-test readiness endpoints.

Included
- /pos-module/offline-sync/reliability-audit page.
- Reliability status endpoint.
- Stress-test scenario registry endpoint.
- Queue integrity verification endpoint.
- Register close pending queue protection summary.
- Stock and Customers ledger reconciliation readiness indicators.
- SQL foundation for reliability tests and integrity snapshots.

Risk Controls Covered
- Browser crash / power failure recovery.
- Internet loss during payment/sync.
- Duplicate sync replay attempts.
- Partial transaction apply protection.
- Register/shift close while offline transactions are pending.
- Stock reconciliation after offline sales/returns.
- Customer ledger duplicate posting prevention.
- High-volume batch sync readiness.
