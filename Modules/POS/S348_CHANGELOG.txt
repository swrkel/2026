POS Standalone S348 - Products & Stock

Added:
- Product register page with Communication Hub style KPI cards, filters, table and actions.
- Add/edit/delete POS products using only POS-owned tables.
- Standalone categories, brands and units table support.
- Stock adjustment page with stock in/out/damage/correction movement types.
