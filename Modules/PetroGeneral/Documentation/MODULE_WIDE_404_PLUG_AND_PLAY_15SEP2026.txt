PetroGeneral - module-wide 404 hardening - 15 Sep 2026

- Supports businesses in the master/system database and businesses in separate tenant databases.
- Registers the installed PetroGeneral route set even if Laravel loaded an older route cache.
- Existing /petro-general/... URLs are re-registered from the installed module for hard-coded AJAX/backward compatibility.
- Named petrogeneral.* routes are mirrored to /petro-general-live/... so route() helpers avoid stale/core route collisions.
- Legacy /petro compatibility remains intact when the old Petro module is disabled.
- po_payment.js is served by PetroGeneral itself, so these pages do not depend on vendor:publish/public modules assets.
- No SQL, migration, route:clear, optimize:clear, or vendor:publish is required.

Deployment: replace Modules/PetroGeneral and Ctrl+F5.
