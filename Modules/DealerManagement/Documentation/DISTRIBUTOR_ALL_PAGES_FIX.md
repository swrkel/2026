# Distributor All Pages Fix

The Distributor/ERP side now always exposes the complete Dealer Management operational menu whenever Distribution is enabled for the business. Page visibility does not depend on whether a dealer record exists. Empty pages show a user-friendly no-data message and populate automatically when dealer data becomes available.

Distributor pages include Dashboard, Dealers, Dealer Outlets, Dealer Users, Dealer Roles, Dealer Stock Balances, Dealer Stock History, Dealer Stock Updates/Confirmations, Re-order Planning, Dealer Orders, Deliveries, Returns, Notifications & Alerts, Reports, Distribution Sync, and Dealer Login.
