IS2216 – Finance Reports / Profit & Loss Fix
8 Sep 2026

SOURCE
- Latest user-supplied FinanceReports.zip
- Changes are confined to FinanceReports.
- No SQL / migration is required.

ROOT CAUSES AND FIXES

1) COST did not agree with COGS Account Book
Original Profit Breakdown calculated cost again from purchase_lines.purchase_price_inc_tax through purchase/sell mappings. This can differ from the amount actually posted to Accounting, including the reported 20,000.00 versus 19,999.82 case.

Fix:
- COST is now sourced from posted COGS account_transactions.
- COGS accounts are resolved from the configured COGS Account Group/default group and compatible account names.
- Debit COGS postings increase cost; credit/reversal postings reduce cost.
- Posted COGS is attributed by sell_line_id / transaction_sell_line_id where available.
- The final COST total is reconciled to the selected-period/location COGS ledger.
- A valid ledger amount with no sell-line attribution is shown as "COGS Ledger Adjustment / Unallocated". It is deliberately not invented into a product/category row.

2) Discount / Profit / Margin
Added Discount to all nine Profit Breakdown tabs and exports.
Revenue is presented before discount so the requested formulas are applied exactly:
- Gross Profit = Revenue - Cost - Discount
- Margin % = Gross Profit / (Revenue - Discount) * 100
Line discount and bill-level discount are included, with fixed bill discounts allocated across lines by revenue.

3) Direct Settlement Other Income
Legacy/Direct settlement Other Income is stored in other_incomes and linked to the finalized sell/settlement transaction, while the legacy posting path credits Sales Income. The Financial Performance Summary now reclassifies that source amount from Sales / Operating Revenue to Other Income. Total Income and Net Profit are unchanged by the reclassification.

4) Date auto-load
Profit & Loss opts into immediate form submission after Date Range Apply/Clear. Other FinanceReports pages retain their previous shared-filter behavior.

5) Toolbar / exports
- Toolbar now targets the Profit Breakdown table explicitly.
- Column Visibility includes header, body and total footer cells.
- CSV / Excel / PDF export the active breakdown instead of the keyed Financial Performance Summary structure.
- PDF opens the printable export in a new tab.
- Print clones the complete P&L report into a direct body print root, fixing parent-section print hiding.
