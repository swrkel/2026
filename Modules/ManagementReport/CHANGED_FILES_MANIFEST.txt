STOCK VALUE STATUS MOVEMENT BREAKDOWN - 25 SEP 2026
===================================================

Changed:
  Services/Reports/Sections/StockValueSectionService.php
    - Replaces the location/store row list with the requested stock-status movement breakdown.
    - Keeps the proven historical closing-stock valuation as Balance Stock and as payload.total.
    - Adds Opening Stock, Purchased Value, Sales Return, Stock Adjustment, Sold and Purchase Return.
    - Stock Adjustment is positive for increase adjustments and negative for reduction adjustments.
    - Opening Stock is the brought-forward value that reconciles the movement formula exactly to Balance Stock.

  Resources/views/daily/sections/stock-value.blade.php
    - Displays the six movement rows in the requested order.
    - Displays Balance Stock as the section total.

Required formula:
  Balance Stock = Opening Stock + Purchased Value + Sales Return + Stock Adjustment - Sold - Purchase Return

Database changes:
  None.

Deployment:
  Replace the ManagementReport module, run php artisan optimize:clear, then hard-refresh the report page.
