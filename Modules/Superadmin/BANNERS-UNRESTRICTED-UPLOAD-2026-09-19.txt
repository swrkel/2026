SUPER ADMIN / SUPER ADMIN SETTINGS / BANNERS
Update: 19 Sep 2026

Banner Add/Edit upload restrictions removed:
- No application-level maximum banner file weight.
- No application-level maximum banner pixel width/height.
- Uploaded banner files are stored at their original dimensions/quality without forced 400x400 resizing.
- Supported image-type validation remains (JPEG, JPG, PNG, GIF, SVG).
- Add still supports multiple banner images in one submission.
- Edit remains a single replacement-image operation.

Note: Web-server/PHP infrastructure limits such as upload_max_filesize, post_max_size,
proxy/body limits, or hosting limits are outside this module and may still apply if configured.
