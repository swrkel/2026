# Products New — Business Product UID V1

Prepared: 15 September 2026

## Purpose

Introduce a permanent Business Product UID without changing or replacing the ERP's existing local numeric product/variation IDs.

## Identity rules

1. Every local `products` row receives its own globally unique `product_uid`.
2. Every local `variations` row receives its own globally unique `variation_uid`.
3. Different tenant databases never share a UID by name, SKU, barcode, brand, unit or any other product property.
4. Different businesses never share a UID by product similarity.
5. Locations/stores inside the same business use the same local product and therefore the same UID. Location is not part of product identity.
6. UIDs are generated by the ERP and are immutable. Forms/imports cannot provide or replace them.
7. Existing `products.id`, `variations.id`, transaction foreign keys, stock rows and stock-transfer relationships remain unchanged.

## Mixed-version compatibility

The module checks for `products.product_uid` and `variations.variation_uid` before using them. Therefore:

- **legacy** tenant: UID columns absent; current ID/SKU logic continues;
- **uid_ready** tenant: nullable UID columns exist; new/edited products can receive UIDs while old rows may remain NULL;
- **backfilled** tenant: all existing product/variation rows have UIDs.

No normal request automatically runs DDL or upgrades another tenant database.

## Central registry

Central sync is disabled unless `PRODUCTSNEW_UID_REGISTRY_CONNECTION` is explicitly set to a dedicated central connection that is not the tenant-repointed default connection.

The central registry stores identity mappings only. It does not store or calculate tenant stock.

Registry identity:

`product_uid -> tenant_key + business_id + tenant_product_id`

There is intentionally no `location_id` in the product registry.

## SQL rollout order

Pilot one non-critical tenant first.

1. `01_TENANT_UID_FOUNDATION_SAFE.sql`
2. Verify application remains normal.
3. `02_TENANT_UID_BACKFILL_EXISTING_SAFE.sql`
4. `04_TENANT_UID_AUDIT_READ_ONLY.sql`
5. On the central/master database, run `03_MASTER_UID_REGISTRY_SAFE.sql` once.
6. Configure a dedicated central Laravel connection only after the master registry exists and connection routing is verified.

Do not mass-run all tenant databases until the pilot is verified.

## Duplicate prevention

Business Product UID complements, rather than replaces, the existing duplicate guard:

- duplicate SKU blocked per business;
- duplicate barcode blocked per business;
- duplicate normalized Single-product name blocked per business;
- import duplicates blocked;
- stock row creation serialized/consolidated by variation + location.

A UID alone cannot detect an attempted duplicate because a new record could always receive a new UID. The business duplicate guard therefore remains mandatory.

## Rollback posture

Because UID columns are additive and legacy local IDs remain authoritative, application rollback is safe. Do not remove UID columns after central registry use begins unless mappings are first archived and all UID-aware integrations are disabled.
