PPD-LIST-TOTAL-007

Issue:
- Petro PD / List Settlement / Total Amount column did not match Settlement Print/Preview Payment Details total.
- Meter sale amount was being added again in the list page calculation.

Fix:
- Updated PetroPDSettlementController List Settlement datatable total_amount column.
- The list total now uses the same Payment Details source as print preview:
  customer loans + cash deposits + cash + card + cheque + credit sales net of discount + expenses + shortage + excess.
- Meter sales and other sales are intentionally excluded from the list total calculation to prevent double counting.
- Added fallback for old settlements without settlement_cash_payments rows using daily_collections only when cash payment rows are missing.

Changed file:
- Modules/PetroPD/Http/Controllers/PetroPDSettlementController.php
