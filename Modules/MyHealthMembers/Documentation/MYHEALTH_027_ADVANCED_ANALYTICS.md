# MYHEALTH_027 - Advanced Analytics & Executive Dashboard

This release adds the My Health analytics layer.

Included:
- Executive dashboard KPIs
- Clinical intelligence summaries
- Operational analytics
- Financial analytics
- Public health analytics
- Date range filters
- Print-ready report pages
- Standalone route/controller/service/view structure

Routes are kept inside the MyHealthMembers module under `Routes/analytics.php`.
