# MYHEALTH_016 Member Portal

Adds a standalone member portal under the My Health module.

Routes:
- myhealth/portal
- myhealth/portal/profile
- myhealth/portal/history
- myhealth/portal/prescriptions
- myhealth/portal/documents

The portal resolves the logged-in user to a My Health member by `myhealth_member_id`, email, or mobile. It does not expose other members' data.
