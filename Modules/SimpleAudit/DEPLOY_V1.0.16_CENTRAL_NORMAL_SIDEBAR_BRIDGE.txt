Simple Audit v1.0.16 - Central Normal Sidebar Bridge
25 Sep 2026

Reason:
The Central session can render the host's normal sidebar on Central pages whose
first URL segment is not /superadmin. Previous releases only inserted Simple Audit
into sidebar-superadmin.blade.php, so the menu could be absent even though routes,
database objects and the Central-only module were all healthy.

Fix:
1. Keeps the dedicated sidebar section:
   resources/views/layouts/partials/sidebar-sections/sidebar-simple-audit.blade.php
2. The dedicated section now self-gates using
   SidebarPermissionUtil::isGenuineSuperAdmin().
3. The same dedicated section is included by both:
   - resources/views/layouts/partials/sidebar-superadmin.blade.php
   - resources/views/layouts/partials/sidebar.blade.php
4. Login As Business and ordinary tenant/business users remain hidden because
   isGenuineSuperAdmin() returns false in those contexts.
5. No SQL/database changes.
