Simple Audit v1.0.14 - Dedicated Central Sidebar Section
25 Sep 2026

Purpose
-------
Move the Central Simple Audit menu into its own host sidebar section file:
resources/views/layouts/partials/sidebar-sections/sidebar-simple-audit.blade.php

Why
---
- Cleaner than embedding module markup inside sidebar-superadmin.blade.php.
- Central-only by construction: only sidebar-superadmin includes it.
- Does not depend on business Manage Side Bar / Manage Page / Role permissions.
- Does not depend on AutomaticModuleRegistry to decide whether the link renders.
- Keeps the tenant/business sidebar free of Simple Audit.

Deploy
------
Extract at Laravel project root and overwrite matching files.
Then run:
  php artisan module:enable SimpleAudit
  php artisan optimize:clear
  php artisan view:clear
  php Modules/SimpleAudit/Tools/check_sidebar.php
  php artisan simple-audit:routes

No SQL changes are required.
