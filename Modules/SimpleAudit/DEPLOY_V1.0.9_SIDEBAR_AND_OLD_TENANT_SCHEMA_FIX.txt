Simple Audit v1.0.9 - Central Sidebar + Older Tenant Schema Compatibility
25 Sep 2026

Fixes
1. Central Super Admin sidebar now uses the same AccessService decision as the Simple Audit route middleware.
   This prevents the page from being accessible while the Simple Audit menu is hidden.
2. Simple Audit remains CENTRAL ONLY. It stays excluded from tenant/business sidebars and business Manage Side Bar.
3. Tenant installer is schema-aware. Older tenant databases that do not have
   account_transactions.location_id (or newer optional audit columns) are supported.
   Business/location are derived from linked purchase/payment transactions when needed.
4. Re-running simple-audit:install is safe after a previous partial failure. Existing sau_ tables are retained and triggers are replaced.

After deployment
php artisan module:enable SimpleAudit
php artisan optimize:clear
php Modules/SimpleAudit/Tools/check_sidebar.php
php artisan simple-audit:install --tenant=100
php artisan simple-audit:diagnose --tenant=100
php artisan route:list | grep simple-audit

Expected
- Central Super Admin: Simple Audit > Purchase Audit visible.
- Business/tenant sidebar: Simple Audit not visible.
- Tenant 100: 5 sau_ tables present and Simple Audit triggers installed.
