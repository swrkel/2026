# AutoService Stage 036 - Customer Experience Portal

Adds customer experience tools to the Auto Service customer portal.

## Included
- Live service progress refresh endpoint.
- Customer callback request form.
- Customer-downloadable job card.
- Customer-downloadable inspection report.
- Customer-downloadable warranty certificate.
- Callback request table and indexes.
- Customer-facing progress/download settings on jobs/settings.
- Permissions for customer experience viewing and management.
