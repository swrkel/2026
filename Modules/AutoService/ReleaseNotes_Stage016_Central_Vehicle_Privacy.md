# AutoService Stage 016 - Central Vehicle Registry Privacy

## Added
- Central vehicle privacy service with separate Owner View and Workshop View.
- Workshop-safe central service history page.
- Workshop view hides previous service place, business/contact details, invoice numbers, payment details and all prices.
- Workshop view shows only service date, mileage, diagnosis/work done, parts/products used and lubricants/oils used.
- Service sync now stores only non-financial product/lubricant details in central parts/oil JSON arrays.

## Owner View
- The current/last registered owner, after OTP verification, can view full lifetime cost and invoice/cost summary.

## Workshop View
- Other workshops can check useful maintenance history without seeing competitor business details or pricing.
