# AutoService Stage 035 – Advanced Vehicle History

## Included
- Advanced Vehicle History search page.
- Vehicle medical-record style detail page.
- Service job history.
- Parts/accessories history with date, qty, unit price, discount, tax, and total.
- Labour history.
- Invoice/payment summary history.
- Odometer timeline.
- Documents/photos history.
- Warranty history hook.
- Timeline events.
- CSV export for vehicle parts/accessories usage.
