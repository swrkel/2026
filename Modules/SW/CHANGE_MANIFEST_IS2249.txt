IS2249 – SW Settlement – 11 Sep 2026

Fixed inside the SW module only; no SQL/migration is required.

1. SW Shifts / Daily Shift / Status filter
   - Uses authenticated tenant business context first.
   - Open / Closed / Settled filtering now supports current numeric and legacy text status values.
   - sw_settlement_shifts is treated as authoritative evidence of Settled status.

2. List SW Shift
   - Increased table cell/heading height by 25%.
   - Total Amount and Settlement Amount headings are shown on two lines.
   - Date column allocation increased by 5% from its previous width.
   - Pump Nos now resolve from settlement pumps, operator-assigned pumps, then active pumps at the shift location.
   - Pump filter follows the same fallback relationship.

3. Settlement / Payments / Expenses
   - Expense-category AJAX now uses the authenticated tenant business, matching the settlement controller.
   - Selecting Expenses refreshes expense_categories so newly added categories appear without reloading the page.

4. Settlement / Payments / Excess
   - Negative Excess values are accepted and saved (example: -11003.13).
   - Negative Excess reduces Total Paid and clears a negative settlement balance correctly.
   - Finance posting keeps the established positive ledger amount with credit direction for Excess.

5. Product New / Stock Center / Available
   - Each newly posted stock-enabled SW sell line now reduces variation_location_details.qty_available.
   - Stock reduction is inside the settlement transaction.
   - A sell-line marker prevents duplicate stock reduction on retries and can repair pre-fix SW sell lines once if Finance posting is retried.
   - Credit Sales remain allocation-only and do not reduce stock a second time.
