IS2253 – SW Settlement – 11 Sep 2026
====================================
Source used: user-supplied latest SW.zip

ISSUES FIXED
------------
1) Petro General / Tank Management / Fuel Tanks
   - SW Settlement meter sales are now posted into Petro's standard tank_sell_lines ledger.
   - The posting uses each pump's configured fuel_tank_id.
   - Multiple pumps drawing from the same tank are aggregated into one tank movement for the settlement transaction.
   - Petro's normal tank-balance calculation can therefore reduce the related tank by the SW sold quantity.
   - The bridge is idempotent on settlement transaction + tank, so a retry does not create a second tank deduction.
   - Business/location/product mapping is validated before posting to prevent cross-business or wrong-tank stock movement.
   - If Petro tank tables are not installed for a tenant, SW continues normally without forcing a Petro dependency.

2) Petro General / Tank Management / Tank Transaction Details + Summary
   - The same SW tank_sell_lines movement is linked to the SW settlement's normal core sell transaction.
   - Existing Petro General transaction reports can therefore read the SW tank movement through their standard source.

3) SW Settlement / New Settlement / Payments / Expenses
   - Expense Categories now resolve against the active session business used by the core Expenses module.
   - The live category endpoint now checks table/column metadata on the current tenant DB connection instead of relying on potentially stale Schema metadata.
   - The Expense Category selector is refreshed once when the settlement page loads, again whenever Expenses is selected, and again when returning to the page after adding a category in another tab/window.
   - Existing no-cache and stale-request protection remains in place.
