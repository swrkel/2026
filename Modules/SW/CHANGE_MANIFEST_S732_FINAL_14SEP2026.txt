ROOT ISSUES CORRECTED
---------------------
1. Payment rows appearing under unrelated payment tabs
   - Removed the multi-tbody display approach.
   - The screen now has exactly ONE visible payment tbody (#sw_pay_rows).
   - Only rows whose payment_method matches the currently selected payment type
     are rendered into the visible DOM.
   - All payment types remain preserved in hidden submission inputs, so tab
     switching cannot lose payments from other types.

2. Payment tabs not opening smoothly / looking disabled
   - Added one authoritative click handler on #sw_sec_payments.
   - Stops legacy/global document-level handlers from immediately undoing the
     selected SW payment tab.
   - ALL eleven SW payment buttons are forced selectable, not only a subset.
   - Added protection against later disabled-class/attribute injection.
   - Active tab remains white with dark text; inactive tabs stay strong/full
     opacity and clearly selectable.

3. Save Settlement visible while Balance is not zero
   - Replaced multiple readiness flags with ONE authoritative state:
     window.swSettlementDataReady.
   - It becomes true only after the selected-shift assemble request, Daily Credit
     Sales load, and Payment Summary load have ALL completed successfully.
   - Save Settlement remains hidden/disabled during loading.
   - After loading, Save is available only when final Balance is within the
     system's 0.005 currency tolerance of zero.
   - Existing server-side zero-balance validation remains in place.

4. Daily Shift Cash open/closed rule
   - Daily Cash remains the authoritative source of Cash for Settlement.
   - While the source shift is OPEN, Daily Cash can be added, edited and removed
     from SW Payments -> Daily Cash.
   - Once the shift is CLOSED, edit/delete/add is blocked server-side.
   - Settlement shows Daily Cash as read-only because settlements consume CLOSED
     shifts and reload authoritative Cash again on final save.
   - DailyCashController now uses the selected session business before the user's
     home business and uses live tenant-database metadata checks instead of the
     Schema facade for the open/closed/action decision.
   - Edit/delete queries are business-scoped through sw_shifts.

5. Multi-business lookup consistency
   - PaymentSummaryController and CreditSaleController now use the selected
     session business first, with auth user business only as a fallback.
