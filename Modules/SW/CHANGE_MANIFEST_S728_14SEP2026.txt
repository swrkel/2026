S 728 - SW Module Settlement - 14 Sep 2026
==========================================

Issue
-----
SW Settlements > New Settlement > Payments:
Cash / Cards / Credit Sales / Shortage / Excess could not reliably be opened.

Root hardening
--------------
1. Payment-type selection is now handled by one delegated, namespaced click
   handler bound before Select2/AJAX initialisation. It continues to work even
   if the hidden settlement section is restored or its live DOM changes.
2. Removed the old direct .sw-pay-btn click binding to prevent double execution.
3. Cash, Card, Credit Sales, Shortage and Excess are explicitly kept enabled
   and free of inherited disabled/aria-disabled states.
4. Credit Sales now explicitly opens its embedded detailed section when chosen,
   and closes it again when another payment type is chosen.
5. Core payment buttons are visually presented as available/clickable rather
   than looking disabled merely because they are inactive.
6. Payment-summary refresh reasserts the enabled state of the five required
   payment types.

Files changed
-------------
Resources/views/settlements/partials/payments_js.blade.php
Resources/views/settlements/partials/section_styles.blade.php

Database
--------
No SQL / migration required.

Scope protection
----------------
No settlement totals, finance posting, shift eligibility, meter sales, other
sales, other income, credit-sale calculations, customer ledger, stock or tank
logic was changed.
