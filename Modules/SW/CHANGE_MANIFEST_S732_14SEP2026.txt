SW MODULE — S732 CORRECTION MANIFEST
Date: 14 Sep 2026
Basis: SW_S731_All_Issues_Fixed_14Sep2026 (latest consolidated SW parcel in this chat)

S732 requirements corrected
===========================
1) SW Settlements / Payment — rows isolated by payment type
   - Replaced the single reusable payment-table body with one dedicated tbody per payment type.
   - Switching Cash/Card/Cheque/Expense/Shortage/Excess/etc. now changes only the visible pane.
   - Hidden form inputs continue to include ALL payments so switching tabs cannot lose another type's rows.
   - Credit Sales keeps its dedicated detailed form/table.

2) Save Settlement — only available at true zero balance
   - Save remains hidden/disabled until a shift is selected, Payment Summary has loaded successfully,
     Daily Credit Sales has loaded successfully, and Balance is exactly zero within currency tolerance.
   - Added a balance MutationObserver fail-safe so stale UI code cannot leave Save visible after balance changes.
   - Existing server-side zero-balance validation remains authoritative.

3) Daily Shift Cash lifecycle
   - SW Payments / Daily Cash rows are editable/deletable only while their SW shift is OPEN.
   - Legacy textual/numeric shift statuses are normalized before deciding whether Edit/Delete is shown.
   - Store/Update/Delete continue to reject closed shifts on the server.
   - Settlement treats Daily Cash as an authoritative source row: closed-shift Daily Cash cannot be removed
     or changed from the Settlement screen.
   - On final settlement save, browser-submitted Cash rows are discarded and reloaded from sw_daily_cash
     for the selected shifts/operator, preventing cached/forged pages from altering closed-shift cash.

4) Payment tab usability / visual clarity
   - Tab switching is lightweight and delegated, so it does not rebuild all payment rows on every click.
   - Payment buttons remain full-opacity and clickable.
   - Active tab uses white background, dark text, strong border; inactive tabs retain clear solid colors.

Recommended verification
========================
A. Payment isolation
   - Open a settlement and add different payment types.
   - Cash tab must show only Cash rows, Cards only Cards, etc.
   - Switch tabs repeatedly: no row should leak to another payment type.

B. Save Settlement gate
   - With any non-zero Balance: Save Settlement must remain hidden/disabled.
   - After all authoritative summaries finish loading and Balance = 0.00: Save Settlement becomes available.

C. Daily Cash open/closed rule
   - On SW Payments / Daily Cash with an OPEN shift: Edit/Delete must be available and additional cash may be added.
   - Close the shift: existing cash must show locked; Edit/Delete and further additions/changes are rejected.
   - In Settlement, Daily Cash rows are read-only.

D. Tabs
   - Click every Payment button repeatedly. Each should open immediately and visibly show its active state.
