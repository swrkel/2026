Settlement SW - Reconfirmation/Settlement Preview + Shift Isolation
Date: 26 Sep 2026

Purpose
- Add the same confirmation discipline used by Petro PD/Petro Direct before saving an SW Settlement.
- Ensure the preview and final print cannot combine rows from another SW Shift/operator/business.

Main safeguards
1. Save Settlement opens a Reconfirmation/Settlement Preview modal first.
2. Preview includes Meter Sales, Other Sales, Other Income, Credit Sales and Payment Details.
3. Confirmation button: "Confirm all the entered details are correct".
4. Preview is rendered from the exact browser draft that is submitted; it does not re-query broad live data.
5. Pump lookup requires the exact selected SW shift(s), business, location and operator.
6. Server save verifies the operator is linked to EVERY selected SW shift and validates submitted pumps against the active business/location/operator context.
7. Changing SW shift clears stale Meter Sale rows before the new shift is loaded.
8. Daily Credit Sales are replaced on shift change, not accumulated from the previous shift.
9. Daily payment rows already use authoritative replacement logic on shift change.
10. After confirmation/save, View/Print uses the saved SW settlement snapshot tables for Meter/Other/Income/Credit rows, not live shift queries.
11. The saved print includes those detail sections and their totals.

Database
- No SQL or migration required.
