IS2297 – Customers Module – 21 Sep 2026

Scope
-----
1. Customer Register > Action > Edit > Credit / Opening Balance > Transaction Date
   - Ensures Bootstrap datepicker is initialized in the AJAX modal.
   - Calendar icon now explicitly opens the picker.

2. Customer Register > Action > Ledger > duplicate Opening Balance
   - Fixes the brought-forward calculation that could count a future Opening Balance
     in the prior-period balance and then display the real Opening Balance again.
   - Existing genuine Opening Balance row is authoritative and no synthetic duplicate
     is added on top of it.
   - Prefers the actual Opening Balance transaction date when legacy contact and
     transaction dates are out of sync.

3. Customer Ledger column widths
   - Description reduced by 15% from its previous width.
   - Payment Status reduced by 5% from its previous width.
   - System Created Date increased by 5% from its previous width.
   - Transaction Date increased by 5% from its previous width.
   - Remaining columns are proportionally balanced so table width remains 100%.

4. Pay Due Amount > Payment Method
   - Removes Credit, Credit Sale(s), Own Card(s), and Pre Payment(s) only from
     the Pay Due action.
   - Advance Payment and other existing workflows retain their prior method list.
   - Server-side validation also rejects those methods for Pay Due.

5. Pay Due Amount > Save
   - Successful AJAX save now instructs Customer Register to perform a full page refresh,
     ensuring row balance, overall due totals, and action state are current.
