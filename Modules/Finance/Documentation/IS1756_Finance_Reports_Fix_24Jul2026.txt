IS1756 - Finance Reports - 24 July 2026
==========================================

Scope
-----
Finance module-only correction for the Finance Reports pages reported as 404:
- Dashboard
- Trial Balance
- General Ledger
- Account Ledger
- Day Book
- Income Statement
- Balance Sheet
- Profit & Loss
- Cash Flow Statement

Main corrections
----------------
1. Added a standalone Finance Reports route file and loaded it from the Finance route loader.
2. Added canonical /finance-reports URLs and compatibility for /finance/reports,
   /finance-report and /finance_reports menu URLs.
3. Added a Finance Reports dashboard and report navigation.
4. Added Finance-owned Account Ledger, Day Book and Cash Flow Statement pages.
5. Kept all data restricted to the logged-in tenant database, business and selected location.
6. Added paginated ledger/day-book/cash-flow queries instead of loading complete histories.
7. Corrected Income Statement to render independently from the Profit & Loss title.
8. Corrected Profit & Loss to load consolidated data when All Locations is selected.
9. Corrected Trial Balance location handling for transaction-linked and direct account postings.
10. Preserved the existing Balance Sheet, Trial Balance and General Ledger implementations
    while exposing them through working Finance Reports URLs.

Deployment
----------
Extract at the Laravel project root and overwrite the included Modules/Finance files.
Then run:
php artisan optimize:clear
php artisan route:clear
php artisan view:clear
php artisan config:clear

No SQL or migration is required.
