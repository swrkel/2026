IS1845 – FINANCE MODULE / DEPOSITS FIX
Date: 31 July 2026

Scope
-----
Finance module only. No Purchase module file is changed or included.

Corrections
-----------
1. Cheque Deposit list now displays exactly: Name, Cheque Number, Cheque Date, Bank and Amount for customer cheque payments, including Pay Due and Bulk Payment records.
2. Deposit to Account is resolved from active bank accounts of the logged-in business and the Select2 dropdown is initialized inside the modal.
3. Cheque Deposit, Cash Deposit, Card Deposit and Fund Transfer now return the exact message “Successfully Saved”. Finance success notices use a green background and white text.
4. Fund Transfer creates explicit business-scoped paired account rows and Account Books display the counterpart account as From/To details.
5. Cheque list loading is Finance-owned, bounded, de-duplicated and no longer depends on customer-payment-information endpoints.

Database
--------
No migration or raw SQL is required.
