S717 v4 - Supplier Advance Payment - Plug and Play

Correct accounting rule:
- Debit: Accounts Payable only.
- Credit: the payment-method account selected by the user only
  (Bank, Cash, Card, Cheque or another configured payment account).
- The payment-method account must never receive the debit leg of the same
  Supplier Advance Payment.

Deployment:
1. Replace/extract the Finance and Suppliers module folders.
2. Refresh the browser (Ctrl+F5 recommended).

No manual SQL import, targeted migration command, or vendor:publish command is
required. Historical affected Supplier advance_payment rows are re-checked
automatically and idempotently in the active tenant/business when Suppliers or
Finance is used.

Safety:
- Only Supplier advance_payment families are considered.
- Accounts Payable is resolved only from the business's Accounts Payable account.
- The payment account must be the actual stored/selected account; the repair does
  not invent or guess a bank/payment account.
- Debit rows wrongly sitting in the selected payment account/Cash are moved to
  Accounts Payable.
- Credit rows stay/move only to the selected payment-method account.
- If the correct target row already exists for the same transaction/payment and
  amount, the duplicate wrong row is removed instead of creating another entry.
