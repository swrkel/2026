Finance List Accounts AJAX live-endpoint correction - 15 Sep 2026

Problem
-------
The collision-proof /finance/list-accounts-live page loaded successfully, but some
installations still returned DataTables Ajax errors for Account Groups and Account
Settings because those tabs continued calling older shared URLs such as
/finance/account-groups/data and /finance/settings.

Fix
---
The Account Groups and Account Settings workflows used by List Accounts now use
unique Finance-owned URLs below /finance/list-accounts-live/.  This avoids old route
cache/core route collisions while preserving the normal auth/session/tenant/master
database middleware.

No SQL, migration or artisan command is required.
