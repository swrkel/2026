IS2078 – Journal – 21 Aug 2026

1. Journal transaction date integrity
- Native Journal date field submits ISO Y-m-d.
- Finance JournalController resolves the date once and persists the same canonical date to:
  journals.date
  account_transactions.operation_date
  transactions.transaction_date
  contact_ledgers.operation_date
  opening-balance account transactions
- Prevents day/month reversal such as 2026-08-01 becoming 2026-01-08.
- Edit/save of an older affected journal re-synchronises its linked ledger dates.

2. Account Book description
- Journal source number now displays JOUR + zero-padded journal_id, e.g. JOUR0003.

3. PetroGeneral Pump Operator ledger
- Restored Finance Journal reader removed by the later PetroGeneral live baseline.
- Reads authoritative journals rows linked by show_in_ledger=pump_operator and pump_operator id.
- Recovery fallback reads linked transactions/contact_ledgers for older tenant data.
- Journal debit/credit rows are rendered in the Pump Operator ledger and included in running balance.
- Current Pumper Management Daily Collection and Excess/Shortage route fixes are preserved.
