S712 - Supplier Payment Advance - 12 Sep 2026

Issue
-----
Supplier Module > List Supplier > Action > Advance Payment
A Bank Transfer advance payment could appear in Finance > Cash Account Book
because a legacy account-transaction path fell back to the Cash account when the
payment account was not available at the exact instant the account row was made.

Fix
---
1. Finance now watches both Core and Finance AccountTransaction saves.
2. It only changes the narrowly identified case:
   - transaction type = advance_payment
   - contact type = supplier
   - payment method = bank transfer
   - current account = Cash / empty
3. Correct-account resolution order:
   a) transaction_payments.account_id (the selected payment account)
   b) transaction_payments.related_account_id (legacy bank-transfer forms)
   c) Business Location default account for bank_transfer
4. Finance never guesses between bank accounts. If no safe non-Cash account can
   be resolved, the row is left unchanged and logged.
5. A TransactionPayment saved hook re-checks the row so event ordering cannot
   leave the entry in Cash.
