IS2067 – Transfer and Deposit – 20 Aug 2026

Scope
-----
Finance module only.
No database migration or raw SQL change is required.

Issues corrected
----------------
1. Finance Module / List Deposits & Transfers
   - Date & Time now displays the saved account_transactions.operation_date with the business date/time format.
   - Legacy null/zero operation_date rows fall back to created_at so the column no longer appears blank.
   - Date-range filtering keeps those legacy fallback rows visible in the correct period.

2. Finance Module / Transfers
   - Normal Account Transfer is now treated as an account transfer, not as a cheque payment.
   - Transfer From / Transfer To / amount are validated server-side.
   - Transfer date is parsed through a hardened multi-format resolver.
   - Paired credit/debit account_transactions are created atomically and linked both ways.
   - Manual transfers bypass the generic duplicate guard so two legitimate same-amount transfers are not collapsed.
   - Cheque/PDC mode on the List Accounts transfer form remains available and separate.
   - Notification/SMS failure no longer makes an already-committed transfer appear to have failed.
   - Successful List Accounts transfer redirects to the List Deposits & Transfers tab.
   - Successful Finance Deposits-page transfer returns to Finance Deposits where the new row is listed.

3. Cheque Deposit customer name
   - Customer Name resolves from the source transaction contact, transaction_payments.payment_for, and (when supported) the parent payment contact used by bulk payments.
   - This is applied to both List Accounts > List Deposits & Transfers and Finance > Deposits list paths.
