CH1 IS2124 – Cheque Deposit – Reworked – 30 Aug 2026

Scope
-----
Finance module only. No Customers/core code changes. No SQL.

Root causes corrected
---------------------
1. The cheque list only accepted one account named exactly "Cheques in Hand".
   Current customer payment pages can legitimately post cheque receipts to an
   operational account linked to the "Cheques in Hand (Customer's)" account
   group. Those saved cheques therefore existed but were filtered out by Finance.

2. Parent/child payment records were tested through a COALESCE method expression.
   A non-cheque parent/direct method could hide a cheque-bearing child payment.
   Each linked payment level is now tested independently.

3. The Cheque No and Amount dropdowns were still calling two legacy
   /customer-payment-information endpoints. Earlier Finance load/crash work had
   intentionally moved Cheque Deposit to one bounded Finance list request, but
   this view had regressed and left the dropdowns dependent on another module.

4. The generic modal shown handler and the inline Cheque Deposit initializer could
   both request the list, creating an unnecessary race. The Cheque Deposit form now
   owns the initial load after its date controls are ready.

Correction
----------
- Finance resolves all active cheque-in-hand accounts for the business from the
  legacy parent name, the Cheques in Hand account group, and its child accounts.
- Saved cheque rows remain business-scoped, debit-only and is_deposited=0.
- The table and both filter dropdowns are populated from the same bounded Finance
  cheque-list response. No extra filter AJAX endpoints are used.
- Changing a date range reloads the unfiltered list and rebuilds both dropdowns.
- Selecting Cheque No or Amount filters the table through the existing Finance list
  endpoint without rebuilding/losing the dropdown options.
- Existing deposit posting logic is unchanged.

Database
--------
No migration or raw SQL required.
