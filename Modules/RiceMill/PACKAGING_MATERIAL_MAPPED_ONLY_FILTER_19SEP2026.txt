Rice Mill - Packaging Material mapped-only filter
Date: 19 Sep 2026

Change:
- Packaging Material displays/selectors now expose only Products explicitly saved in:
  Rice Mill > Settings > Product Category Mapping > Select Packaging Material.
- The Product selector in that Settings section intentionally continues to show eligible Products New products so new mappings can still be added.
- Settings > Material Usage Mapping shows only mapped Packaging Materials.
- Standalone Material Usage Mapping dropdown/list shows only mapped Packaging Materials.
- Rice Mill > Packaging Materials list shows only mapped Products.
- Existing legacy/manual rcm_packaging_materials records are NOT deleted; they are only hidden from these mapped-only workflows.
- Existing stock, status, notes and movement history remain untouched.
- Server-side Material Usage Mapping validation rejects unmapped Packaging Material IDs.

No database migration or SQL is required.
After deployment run:
php artisan optimize:clear
php artisan view:clear
