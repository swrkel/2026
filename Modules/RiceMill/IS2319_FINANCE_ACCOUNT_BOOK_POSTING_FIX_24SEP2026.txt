IS2319 – Rice Mill – 24 Sep 2026

Scope
-----
1. Payment Method -> Payment Account resolver corrected for Purchase Order,
   Receive Paddy, Milling / Production, and Sales / Dispatch.
2. Rice Mill financial and stock movements now write to the ERP standard
   transactions/account_transactions tables so Finance > List Accounts >
   Account Book shows the related entries.

Payment Account resolver
------------------------
- Super Admin / Manage New Account Group mapping remains authoritative.
- Only open, non-main Account Books in the mapped Account Group are returned.
- Location-scoped, 'all', blank, and NULL location Account Books are supported.
- If old location data is stale, fallback is restricted to the SAME mapped
  Account Group; it never reinterprets the Account Group id as another account.

Finance posting
---------------
- Purchase Order: Product stock Account Book debit + selected payment Account
  credit (standard Purchase flow), or Current Liability credit for Credit Purchase.
- Receive Paddy: Paddy stock Account Book debit + mapped Current Liability credit;
  any payment debits the liability and credits the selected Payment Account.
- Milling / Production: Paddy input stock is credited, Rice/By-product output stock
  is debited at production cost, with production overhead kept in the mapped
  liability; payment clears that liability through the selected Payment Account.
- Sales / Dispatch (on approval): Accounts Receivable / Sales Income and
  Finished Goods / COGS Account Books are posted. Customer payment debits the
  selected Payment Account and credits Accounts Receivable.

No new table or column is required for IS2319.
All code changes are contained inside Modules/RiceMill.
