Rice Mill - Out Put Type filter adjustment - 21 Sep 2026

1. Product Category now supports multiple selection and shows all Products New top-level Product Categories.
2. Product Sub Category / Product is compulsory and supports multiple selection.
3. When Product Categories are selected, only their linked Product Sub Categories are shown.
4. The Output Products checkbox list remains hidden/empty until at least one Product Sub Category is selected.
5. The Output Products checkbox list then shows only Products linked to the selected Product Sub Categories.
6. Server-side validation rejects Output Products outside the selected Product Sub Categories.
7. Output Product eligibility remains limited to Products under Rice and By Products, as previously requested.
8. Only checked Output Products are mapped and shown in Mill Production.
9. No database change is required.
