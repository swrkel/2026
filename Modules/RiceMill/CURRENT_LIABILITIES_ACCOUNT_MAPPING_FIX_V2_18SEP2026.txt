Rice Mill - Current Liabilities Account Mapping Fix v2 - 18 Sep 2026

Problem fixed:
The previous Rice Mill query looked for an account_groups row literally named
"Current Liabilities". In the Finance/List Accounts hierarchy, Current Liabilities
is an Account Type/Sub Type. Account Groups beneath it are linked through:

  account_groups.account_type_id -> account_types.id

List Accounts are then linked through either/both:

  accounts.account_type_id -> account_types.id
  accounts.asset_type      -> account_groups.id

The Rice Mill Payment Account dropdown now returns every open account belonging
to Current Liabilities, including accounts linked through any Account Group below
Current Liabilities. It also supports child Account Types under Current Liabilities.

No SQL/database change is required.
