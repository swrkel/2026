Rice Mill - Products New Packaging Material Selection - 19 Sep 2026

Scope
-----
Rice Mill > Settings > Product Category Mapping now includes a new
"Select Packaging Material" section.

Behaviour
---------
1. Product Category is optional and comes from the live Products New category master.
2. Product Sub Category is optional. If Category is selected, only its linked Sub Categories are shown.
3. Product is required and comes from the live Products New product master.
4. Product list is automatically filtered by the optional Category/Sub Category selections.
5. All three dropdowns use the module searchable Select2 standard: type-to-filter,
   scrollable dropdown and keyboard Up/Down navigation.
6. Saved selections are listed on the same Settings page.
7. Saving a Product automatically creates/updates its corresponding Rice Mill Packaging Material.
8. Rice Mill > Packaging Materials auto-syncs saved selections before listing, so saved
   Products always appear there and are available for Material Usage Mapping.
9. Existing Packaging Material stock quantity, Active/Inactive status and user note are preserved.
10. Selected Products New Product ids and internal Packaging Material ids are stored in
    the existing rcm_settings.settings JSON. No database schema change is required.

Products New source
-------------------
Categories/Sub Categories: tenant categories table.
Products: tenant products table.
Product ID: exact products.id used by Products New.
Product Code: products.sku when available.
Unit: resolved from products.unit_id to units.short_name / actual_name / name when available.

Deployment
----------
Replace the RiceMill module with this parcel or upload the changed files.
Then run:
  php artisan optimize:clear
  php artisan view:clear

No SQL import is required.
