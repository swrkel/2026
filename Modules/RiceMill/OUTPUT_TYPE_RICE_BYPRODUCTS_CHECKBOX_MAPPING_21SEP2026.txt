Rice Mill - Out Put Type Product Mapping Update - 21 Sep 2026

Scope
-----
Rice Mill / Settings / Product Category Mapping / Out Put Type

Changes
-------
1. Eligible Output Products now come only from Products New top-level Product Categories:
   - Rice
   - By Products
   Products linked through descendant Product Sub Categories are included.
2. Output Product selection is now a scrollable checkbox box instead of a multi-select Product dropdown.
3. Product Category and Product Sub Category / Product remain optional on-screen filters only.
4. Type-to-filter search is available inside the Output Products box.
5. Select Visible and Clear Visible actions are provided for faster bulk selection.
6. Saving replaces the previous Output mapping with the exact checked Product list.
   Unchecked Products stop appearing in Mill Production -> Output Type immediately.
7. Existing/stale Output mappings outside Rice / By Products are ignored by Production for safety.
8. Added Out Put Type Products remain listed in the same Settings section.

Database
--------
No database schema change. Mapping remains stored in rcm_settings.settings JSON.
