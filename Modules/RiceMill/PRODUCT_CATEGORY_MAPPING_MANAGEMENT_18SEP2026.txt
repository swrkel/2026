Rice Mill - Product Category Mapping management update
18 Sep 2026

Changes:
- Added mappings are displayed in the same Settings / Product Category Mapping tab.
- Saved Paddy and Rice mappings show Product Category, Payment Account, Status and Action.
- Edit is available and moves the user to the same on-page mapping form.
- Delete is intentionally not provided.
- User can Disable/Enable Paddy and Rice mappings independently.
- Disabling keeps the saved Product Category and Payment Account; nothing is deleted.
- Disabled category mappings are not used for new Paddy Variety / Rice Product selections.
- A disabled Paddy mapping is also blocked from being used for new Purchase Order payment posting.
- Existing historical records are untouched.

Database changes: NONE. The enabled/disabled state is stored in the existing rcm_settings.settings JSON.
