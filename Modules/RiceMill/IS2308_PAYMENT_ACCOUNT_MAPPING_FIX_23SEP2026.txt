IS2308 – Rice Mill – Payment Account Mapping Fix – 23 Sep 2026

Scope
- Purchase Order / Purchase Payment
- Receive Paddy / Payment
- Milling & Production / Payment
- Sales – Dispatch / Payment

Fix
- Payment Method account mapping is read from business_locations.default_payment_accounts.
- The stored token from Super Admin / All Businesses / Manage New is treated as an Account Group id when that group exists.
- Only open, non-main Account Books in the mapped Account Group are returned.
- Account Books are filtered for the selected Business Location, while business-wide (all/NULL) books remain available.
- When the mapped Account Group exists but has no linked Account Books, the system returns no accounts instead of incorrectly falling back to an unrelated Accounts.id with the same number.
- Legacy direct Accounts.id mappings remain supported only when no matching Account Group exists.

Database
- No database/SQL change required.
