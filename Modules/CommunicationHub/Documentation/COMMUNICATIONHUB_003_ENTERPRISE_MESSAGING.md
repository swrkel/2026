# COMMUNICATIONHUB_003 – Enterprise Messaging Engine

This release keeps CommunicationHub fully standalone while adding a structured enterprise messaging engine.

## Included

- Provider management for SMS, Email, WhatsApp and Push.
- Template management with placeholders.
- Message queue with pending, processing, sent, failed, retrying and cancelled statuses.
- OTP center foundation.
- Delivery tracking table.
- Delivery reports and CSV export.
- Standalone audit page.

## Independence Rules

CommunicationHub does not depend on the legacy SMS module, legacy Wallet module, My Health, or any business module. Integration with future DigitalWallet is intentionally through an interface only.
