# RestaurantNew Administrator Guide

## Administrator setup checklist
- Enable RestaurantNew from Super Admin.
- Configure restaurant settings per business/location.
- Create dining areas and tables.
- Configure kitchen sections and order routing.
- Create menu categories, menu items, variants, modifiers, recipes, and prices.
- Configure waiter, cashier, kitchen, manager, and executive permissions.
- Configure tax, service charge, payment methods, and bill/KOT print templates.
- Configure inventory ingredients, recipes, wastage, and stock rules.
- Configure delivery zones, riders, CRM/loyalty, online ordering, and QR menu settings if used.

## Critical workflow
1. Waiter or cashier creates sale/order.
2. Kitchen receives all orders in the kitchen display screen.
3. Kitchen prints/reprints KOT or bill copy as permitted.
4. Kitchen updates received/preparing/ready/served status.
5. Cashier finalizes bill and payment.
6. Inventory and reports update according to RestaurantNew rules.
